# 🛒 Enterprise Retail Customer Intelligence Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Enterprise Retail Customer Intelligence Dashboard** is an end-to-end Data Analytics project designed to analyze retail business performance and provide actionable business insights using **SQL, Python, and Power BI**.

The project covers the complete analytics lifecycle, including data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), SQL-based business problem solving, data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive dashboard development.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to help business stakeholders monitor and improve retail performance by analyzing:

- 💰 Sales & Revenue Performance
- 👥 Customer Behavior & Segmentation
- 🛍️ Product Performance
- 🏪 Store Performance
- 🎯 Marketing Campaign Effectiveness

The dashboard enables management to make informed, data-driven decisions through interactive visualizations and business insights.

# 📊 Key Business Insights

- Grocery department generated the highest revenue.
- Homeowners contributed nearly 67% of total revenue.
- National brands outperformed private brands.
- Weekly revenue remained consistently strong.
- Top product categories contributed significantly to total sales.
